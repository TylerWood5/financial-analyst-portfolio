# Silver Sands Resort & Casino Monthly Management Report

## Business Problem

Resort leadership required a monthly operating review package that consolidated financial performance, inventory costs, maintenance expenses, and operational KPIs into a single executive reporting tool.

The objective was to provide management with a clear view of financial results, budget performance, operational trends, and key business drivers to support decision-making.

## Solution

Developed a multi-tab Monthly Management Report that combines financial analysis, variance reporting, inventory and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) review, maintenance reporting, KPI trend analysis, and executive dashboard visualizations.

The report is structured to simulate the type of management package that would be reviewed by a General Manager, Finance Director, Controller, or Operations Director during a monthly operating review meeting.

## Key Findings

- Revenue totaled approximately $47.3 million during the reporting period.
- Actual operating costs exceeded budget by approximately 3.9%.
- Housekeeping generated the highest departmental variance at approximately $148,128.
- Average occupancy reached approximately 88.2%.
- Dairy represented the largest COGS category at approximately $58,291.
- Gaming Operations generated the highest maintenance expenditure at approximately $759,059.
