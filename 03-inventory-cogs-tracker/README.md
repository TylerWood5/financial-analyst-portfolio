# Silver Sands Resort & Casino Inventory & COGS Tracker

## Business Problem

Food and Beverage leadership needed visibility into inventory purchases, product usage, vendor spend, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) in order to better understand operating costs and support inventory management decisions.

## Solution

Built an inventory and COGS reporting model using beginning inventory balances, transaction-level inventory activity, and vendor master data. Developed inventory analysis, cost calculations, PivotTable reporting, and dashboard visualizations to identify spending trends and key cost drivers.

## Skills Demonstrated

- XLOOKUP
- SUMIFS
- Inventory Analysis
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Reporting
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Vendor Spend Analysis
- Dashboard Development
- Data Modeling
